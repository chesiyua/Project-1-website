Siyuan Chen —DESN30146 Physical Computing. In this project, I learned about physical computing through Arduino, ProtoPie, and TouchDesigner. I completed three activities and did some research. Finally, I chose Smart Lighting Scenes as my project concept.

Module 1

Activity Research

In Module 1, I learned how to use Arduino to control lights, read sensor data, and connect physical actions to digital outputs. In Activity 1, I controlled an LED with code and Serial Monitor. In Activity 2, I sent sensor data to a phone app with ProtoPie. In Activity 3, I used a knob to control a visual effect in TouchDesigner.

Research Activity

Consumer Trend Research

I researched two smart home trends: Smart Climate Control and Smart Lighting. I used NielsenIQ and EMARKETER to learn about these technologies.

Personas Research

I created two personas, David and Emily, based on my research. David wants to save money on heating and cooling, while Emily wants to control the lighting in her room more easily.

HMI Research

I looked at the problems David and Emily face in their daily lives and wrote two HMW questions for each of them

Project Path

I chose the Design Path because I want to make something useful in daily life. I also decided to work individually so I can test my ideas at my own pace.

Project 1 Concept

Project 1 Concept

My final concept is Smart Lighting Scenes. I plan to use Arduino and a light sensor to adjust LED brightness automatically, while also allowing users to control the lights manually
